## 3D fixtures & sewing tools (Design3D)
- **v1.7** From a trial design of an inspection table with an LED light by a new IE user (all by mouse and typing): **Smart adding**: a new part lands on top of the selected part or face (plates too) and is scaled to it (x1 to x10, `scalePart`). **Tables**: buttons moved to the top of the left column as compact chips; an empty design gets a real table standing on the floor (counted in list / weight / PDF / CAD), otherwise a reference table underneath; changing the height of a reference table keeps its top at 0; button renamed "Về cao độ 0 (Z=0)". **Fixed**: the fillet / chamfer dropdown went blank after an edit. **Ruler modes**: straight line, height only, horizontal only. **PDF**: editable document title (field in the export dialog, saved in the project); front and side views draw a table with legs and top instead of one solid box. Tests 106 checks. Manuals updated.
- **v1.6** **Sketch and extrude** (`sketch.js`): on a selected flat horizontal face (top / bottom) press "Vẽ phác trên mặt này": the view looks along the face normal, draw rectangles, circles and polygons on the face plane (snap to part vertices and grid, numeric editing), then extrude: add a new free-shape solid on the face, or cut into the part (through cut, or a shallower cut that splits the part into two stacked layers). **Curved faces** are now picked whole: surfaces are grown over triangles whose normals turn less than about 11 degrees (a cylinder wall, a fillet), and a flat top face with a fillet is still a flat face. **Rounded edges**: corner radius also for blocks; bevel-based fillet / chamfer of the top and bottom edges (`er`, `em`) for plates, blocks and free shapes with the outline size kept; round convex / concave corners of free shapes (Clipper opening / closing). Not yet: sketch on side or curved faces (the model extrudes along Z only). Tests 94 checks. Manuals updated.
- **v1.5** **Pick face** (button ▦ / key F, Onshape style): the flat face under the pointer lights up (coplanar triangles of the mesh), click selects it (orange, with a placement point); the shape buttons then add the new part ON the face: top = standing on it, bottom = hanging under it, side = touching it (rotated so its depth axis follows the face normal); the face is cleared when its part changes. **Ruler highlight**: the vertex, edge middle or edge (point closest to the mouse ray) near the pointer is highlighted in orange with the edge length; edges come from the part meshes, hidden ones are ignored by a ray test. **Lock position** (button 🔒 / key L / panel button / lock all): locked parts cannot be dragged, nudged, rotated or have X, Y, Z changed (size and holes stay editable), shown with 🔒, saved in the project (`lock`). Tests 83 checks. Manuals updated.
- **v1.4** **Project folder**: the user picks a folder once (File System Access API, handle kept in IndexedDB `design3d`); Save / Ctrl+S writes `<name>.fixture3d.json` straight into it, no download; Open lists the projects of the folder (newest first) with "Open another file" and "Change folder"; folder name on the 📁 button. Browsers without folder access still download the file. `project.js`. Tests 66 checks (mocked folder). Manuals updated.
- **v1.3** **Join shapes that are close**: with two or more shapes selected, the panel shows the gap between them and the button "Nối thành một hình" joins them into one connected free shape (closing = grow then shrink by the smallest radius that connects them, up to a typed largest gap, default 20 mm; holes of the plain union are kept; same Z and thickness needed). `joinWorld()` in geom.js. Tests 58 checks.
- **v1.2** **Sample tables** (sewing 1200x600x750, work, cutting, small; kind `table` = top + 4 legs, top thickness editable) placed under the design (table top at Z=0). New flag `ref` ("reference only", on for tables, tick for any part): shown in the 3D view and the PDF 3D picture but not counted in the parts list, numbering, weight, PDF drawings and CAD files (`realParts()`). **Ruler** (button 📏 / key M): click two points, snap to part corners, hole centres and free-shape vertices (14 px), shows length and dX/dY/dZ, list in the right panel, saved in the project (`ST.meas`), printed in the PDF 3D picture; mm scale marks along the X and Y axes. **Picture to 3D** (`trace.js`): photo / scan -> blur, Otsu or manual threshold, optional invert and fill of closed outlines, boundary tracing, Douglas-Peucker smoothing, speck filter, hole nesting by Clipper; the real Length (or Width) is typed and the other side follows the picture ratio; result = free-shape part with thickness and material. Fixed: the sample table buttons did nothing (no click handler on that list). Tests 55 checks (table button + edit, ruler by mouse clicks with snap, a generated picture traced to 150 x 100 x 4 with one hole). Manuals updated.
- **v1.1** Multi-select (Ctrl / Shift + click in the picture or the list, drag moves the group). **Merge / cut**: union, subtract (first selected minus the others) and intersection of the shapes (outlines with holes, rounded corners) into one free-shape part (`kind:'poly'`, rings in `polys`), done with Clipper (vendor/clipper.js, Boost licence, integer maths 0.001 mm); union needs the same Z and thickness. **Offset**: grow / shrink the outer outline or the holes / windows of a part by an even distance (round joins), optionally keeping the original as a copy. New parts: wheel (tire + hub, placed under the selected part), U handle, magnet, screw. Free shapes are scaled by Length / Width, appear in the PDF (plate drawings), weight uses their area. **CAD export** (`cad.js`): STL (binary), OBJ, DXF 3D (3DFACE), DXF 2D top view and DXF flat plates for laser / CNC (R12 ASCII, true arcs for rounded rectangles and circles, layers OUTLINE / HOLES / LABEL / INFO with design name, IE name and date). DWG and STEP are not written (proprietary / B-rep): open the DXF and save as DWG in AutoCAD or ODA File Converter. Tests extended (41 checks, DXF read back with ezdxf when installed). Manuals updated.
- **v1.0** New offline tool `design3d/Design3D.html` (built from `src/design3d` by `tools/build_design3d.py`; three.js and the Crystal logo inlined, no network) to design sewing jigs, templates, seam guides and aids in 3D, for garment engineers with no 3D knowledge (not a line layout: that is Layout Sim). Three steps shown on screen (add parts, adjust, export PDF), big part buttons (plate, plate with window / 4 holes / slot, guide rail, stop block, block, round pin, ramp), drag in the 3D view with 1 / 5 / 10 mm snap, numeric size / position / rotation, plates with round holes, slots and rectangular windows (corner radius), materials with estimated weight, optional reference machine bed with the needle, undo / redo, save / open `.fixture3d.json`, four ready examples, VI / EN. PDF export (A4 landscape, no library): Crystal Martin Vietnam logo and name, design name, **IE name (required, remembered)**, style, purpose, notes, revision, date, numbered 3D picture, parts list, three-view drawing with dimensions and scale, plate drawings with a hole coordinate table, signature boxes; live preview with page choice; pages are images so Vietnamese text is exact, the IE name is also the PDF author. Test `tests/design3d/smoke.py` (turnkey). Manual `manual/design3d.html` / `.en.html`.
